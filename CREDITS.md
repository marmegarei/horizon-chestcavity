# Credits and licenses

This mod is a port to Horizon. Its textures, organ names, organ scores, chest cavity
types and recipes come from three Minecraft mods; `tools/import.py` copies them from
`vendor/` (the upstream repositories, at the commits below, not included here).

| Source | Author | Commit | License | What comes from it |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| [Chest Cavity](https://github.com/Tigereye504/chestcavity) (branch `1.20.1`) | Tigereye504 | `3f28dfa` | no license file upstream, see below | most organs, food, cleavers, the Chest Opener; organ and type data; recipes; the score rules in `native/shared/src/effects.rs` |
| [Chest Cavity Beyond](https://github.com/zhaijineet/Chest-Cavity-Beyond) | zhaijineet | `84a7a1e` | MIT, see `licenses/chest-cavity-beyond-MIT.txt` | basalt, elder, sculk, breeze, slime, magma and other new organs, their scores and types |
| [Chest Cavity Astro Adds](https://github.com/A5TR0spud/chest-cavity-astro-adds) | A5TR0spud | `5f31a0c` | CC0-1.0 in its `LICENSE` file (see `licenses/chest-cavity-astro-adds-LICENSE.txt`), but CC BY-NC-SA 4.0 in its `fabric.mod.json` | the add-on organs (abyssal, steel, copper, gold, tumors, canisters, ...) and their recipes |

## Chest Cavity (Tigereye504)

The upstream repository has no license file. Its `fabric.mod.json` says `CC0-1.0`, which is the
value of the Fabric example mod it started from, and Chest Cavity Beyond's README calls the
textures it reuses from Chest Cavity "Apache License 2.0", copyright 2024 Tigereye504. Neither is
a statement from the author in a license file. The author's work is included here with the
permission of Tigereye504, as reported by the owner of this repository. If that changes, the
affected files will be removed.

## Chest Cavity Astro Adds (A5TR0spud)

The two licenses disagree, so everything that comes from it is treated as the stricter one,
[CC BY-NC-SA 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-nc-sa/4.0/): credited to A5TR0spud,
not for commercial use, shared under the same license. That is its 52 textures in
`assets/chestcavity/textures/item/` and the items, recipes and organ scores that
`tools/import.py` takes from `ccastroadds` (the items whose ids `data/chestcavity/horizon/item/`
lists from it).

## What is generated

`assets/`, `data/` and `native/shared/src/organs.rs` are generated by `tools/import.py` from the
three mods above and keep their licenses. They are not under this repository's MIT license.
